My tiles (offline downloads)

Area downloads use your own tile source: a tile server you host, or a provider plan whose terms allow offline caching. The free community maps (OpenStreetMap, OpenTopoMap, Esri) don't allow bulk downloads, so Charta only caches what you view from them. Put {z}, {x}, {y} (and optionally {s}) in the URL.
